    05Selected Work

    Problems, engineered end to end.

    Every engagement follows the same arc — challenge, approach, solution, outcome. Two representative structures are shown below.

    // representative engagements, anonymised — detailed references available on request

    Engagement · OPS-01Scope · Platform / Integration / Automation
    01

    The Challenge

    A growing operations team coordinated every order through spreadsheets, email and phone calls. Data lived in silos, errors were common, and nobody had a real-time view of the business.

    02

    The Approach

    We began with the process, not the technology — mapping how work actually flowed and where it stalled. Then we designed a phased plan the business could adopt without pausing operations.

    03

    The Solution

    A custom web platform with role-based access, integrated with the tools the team already used. Orders, inventory and reporting moved into one system, with automated handoffs replacing manual bottlenecks.

    04

    The Outcome

    One source of truth replaced scattered spreadsheets. Manual steps dropped sharply, errors surfaced before they reached customers — and the platform now scales to new sites without adding friction.

    Engagement · SEC-02Scope · Assessment / Remediation / Retest
    01

    The Challenge

    A customer-facing application was preparing for a major release — with a development history that left its security posture unknown and its team exposed.

    02

    The Approach

    A structured security assessment: threat-surface mapping, an OWASP-aligned review of code and configuration, and a remediation plan prioritised by real risk — not generic checklist items.

    03

    The Solution

    We remediated the vulnerabilities at the source — authentication flaws, injection risks and API access control — hardened the environment, and established secure patterns for future development.

    04

    The Outcome

    Every finding was fixed, verified and retested. The application shipped with a documented security baseline — and the team kept the practices that got it there.
